# Social Media Intelligence (SOCMINT) Workflow

## Objective
Systematic collection, correlation, and analysis of publicly available social media presence to understand organizational structures, brand sentiment, communication patterns, and network graphs.

---

## Intelligence Lifecycle

```
TARGET
  │  [Profile / Hashtag / Community / Keyword]
  ▼
DISCOVERY
  │  Identify presence across relevant social networks and public forums
  ▼
ENUMERATION
  │  Extract public posts, follower connections, mentions, and media uploads
  ▼
CORRELATION
  │  Analyze temporal activity patterns, social network graphs, and key influencers
  ▼
VERIFICATION
  │  Distinguish authentic accounts from bots, sockpuppets, and parody entities
  ▼
DOCUMENTATION
     Archive key posts, network diagrams, sentiment matrices, and activity timelines
```

---

### Phase 1: Target Scoping & Account Mapping
1. Identify primary official accounts and public channels.
2. Map secondary platforms (Mastodon, Telegram channels, Discord servers, Reddit).

### Phase 2: Content & Post Extraction
1. **Historical Timeline**:
   - Extract public updates, replies, retweets, and quoted content.
2. **Media Analysis**:
   - Collect attached images, video uploads, audio files, and external links.
3. **Hashtag & Keyword Tracking**:
   - Monitor specific campaign hashtags, event tags, or organizational keywords.

### Phase 3: Network & Graph Analysis
1. **Follower / Following Topology**:
   - Identify closely connected accounts, mutual followers, and frequent interactors.
2. **Communication Clusters**:
   - Detect sub-communities, bot networks, and astroturfing campaigns.

### Phase 4: Verification & Bot Detection
1. **Account Velocity**:
   - Check account creation date vs total tweet/post count (e.g., >1000 posts/day suggests automation).
2. **Profile Completeness**:
   - Stock profile photos, autogenerated handles (e.g., Name12345678), default bios.

### Phase 5: Evidence Preservation
1. Preserve full-page captures and individual post permalinks using archival services.
2. Document engagement statistics and timeline records.

---

## Ethical & Legal Boundaries
- Collect strictly from publicly available profiles.
- Do not create deceptive accounts to infiltrate private groups.
- Avoid mass harvesting of private personal data.
