# Corporate & Organizational Intelligence Workflow

## Objective
Methodology for conducting corporate intelligence on commercial entities, non-profits, and organizations using official business registries, regulatory filings, intellectual property databases, and open datasets.

---

## Intelligence Lifecycle

```
TARGET
  │  [Company Name / Registration Number / Domain]
  ▼
DISCOVERY
  │  Query official company registries (SEC, Companies House, Handelsregister)
  ▼
ENUMERATION
  │  Extract officers, shareholders, parent entities, subsidiaries, and addresses
  ▼
CORRELATION
  │  Map corporate group hierarchy, trademark filings, patents, and job postings
  ▼
VERIFICATION
  │  Confirm active commercial status, annual filings, and regulatory compliance
  ▼
DOCUMENTATION
     Build corporate family tree, officer relationship graph, and dossier
```

---

### Phase 1: Official Registry Inquiries
1. **National Corporate Registrars**:
   - UK: Companies House (directors, PSCs, accounts).
   - US: SEC EDGAR (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K filings for public entities), state Secretary of State offices.
   - EU: OpenCorporates, national gazettes, and commercial registers.
2. **Entity Verification**:
   - Official legal name, incorporation date, registered office, status (Active/Dissolved).

### Phase 2: Leadership & Ownership Structure
1. **Directors & Officers**:
   - Current and historical directors, beneficial owners, and cross-directorships.
2. **Shareholder & Subsidiary Mapping**:
   - Ultimate parent company, joint ventures, and international subsidiaries.

### Phase 3: Commercial & Operational Footprint
1. **Intellectual Property**:
   - Trademarks (WIPO, USPTO, EUIPO).
   - Patent applications (Google Patents, Espacenet).
2. **Hiring Intelligence**:
   - Current open positions to infer technical stack, operational expansion, and locations.
3. **Procurement & Government Contracts**:
   - Public tender databases (SAM.gov, TED Europe).

### Phase 4: Verification & Graph Generation
1. Validate corporate linkages through multiple independent official filings.
2. Create visual organization charts showing corporate family trees.

---

## Ethical & Legal Boundaries
- Rely exclusively on public records and open source disclosures.
- Comply with all corporate privacy and financial reporting regulations.
